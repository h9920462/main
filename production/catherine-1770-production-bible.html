8 · Aesthetic direction

Photorealistic prestige period drama shot on two cameras. The on-camera lines are the empress's own selfie: a handheld ultra-wide action camera held at arm's length, low angle, slight tilt, barrel-distorted rooms curving around her deadpan face, with servants and courtiers standing silently in the depth of the frame. Everything else is a calm, centred 35mm period-drama camera with shallow focus, often looking through window bars, curtains or doorways. Light is soft and natural: overcast window daylight (or cold snow-glow before dawn) as the base, with candles and fire as warm accents only. The palette is muted: deep crimson, antique gold, ivory lace and cool grey-blue stone, with saturated red lips as the strongest colour. Textures are real: skin pores, lace weave, brocade thread, frost on glass, powder in the air, fine film grain. The world is lavish, exact and serious; only Catherine is funny.
