1 · How to use

  1. Takes. Each card in section 4 is one generation. Make the start image with the image prompt, grade it, save it as Gxx_START.png, then paste the video prompt and set the length on the card (6, 10 or 15 seconds).
  2. Selfie takes carry Catherine's on-camera lines with timed beats, so each line lands inside the take. Close-ups of props and dogs are written as Catherine turning or tilting her camera, so they stay inside the same continuous take instead of becoming separate clips.
  3. Cinematic takes are generated with no speech and quiet ambience. Their narration comes from Google AI Studio (sections 2 and 3). Each voice-over file is named after its take (VO_G04 goes over G04) and is written to fit inside it.
  4. Edit-only cards (the two montages) need no generation, only their voice-over file.
  5. Prompts that say "as in @G09" mean: attach the start image you saved as G09.
  6. In the edit: place the takes in G order, drop each VO file at the start of its take, and cut cinematic takes into the selfie takes where the voice-over continues.

2 · Voice setup in Google AI Studio

Open Google AI Studio, choose Generate speech, set mode to Single-speaker audio, and use a Gemini TTS model (the Pro TTS model gives the best acting; Flash TTS is faster).

Pick: Gacrux

Google's "Mature" voice

A grown woman's voice with weight and calm authority. Lower and richer than most of the female voices, so it reads as a 41-year-old empress rather than a young narrator.

Backup: Kore

Google's "Firm" voice

Clearer and more commanding, slightly younger and less warm. Use it if Gacrux sounds too soft for you.

Backup: Sulafat

Google's "Warm" voice

Warmer and gentler. Good for the fond lines (dogs, Orlov), but less dry for the jokes.
